Bible Study Method

The S.O.A.P Approach

A structured framework to deepen your comprehension and application of the living Word.

S

Scripture

Read the assigned passage with an open heart. Note down words or verses that resonate with your spirit.

O

Observation

Understand what it means. What was the context? What is God communicating to the original audience?

A

Application

Apply it directly to your life. How can you live out these instructions in your modern, everyday routine?

P

Prayer

Respond to God in deep conversation. Ask for the grace and strength to embody what He has revealed to you.
